Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything mill owners usually ask before switching.

How much does it cost to install ProMiller? +
For mills producing 40+ TPD, we install the complete WonderMill + ProMiller system at our cost — you only share savings during the payback period, typically 12-18 months.
Will there be downtime during installation? +
No. Sensors and hardware are fitted around your existing production schedule — most mills go live within two weeks with zero lost production days.
Is my mill's data secure? +
Yes. All data is transmitted over a secure, encrypted connection and stored on private cloud infrastructure accessible only to your team and authorized ProMiller support staff.
What happens after the payback period ends? +
Once payback is complete, 100% of the power savings go to you, and you own the WonderMill + ProMiller system outright with no ongoing fees.
Do I need technical staff to run it? +
No specialized IT staff needed. The dashboard is built for mill operators — our team also trains your existing staff during onboarding.
